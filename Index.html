MANDATORY MDSO PARTNER POLICY

Operating safely and cohesively is the top priority for deputies across Miami-Dade County. To protect personnel and maintain operational control, the partner policy must be strictly observed at all times on patrol.

2 or More Units Online

If there are 2 or more Deputies or Corporals on duty, you are required to partner up and operate from a single patrol vehicle before heading out on patrol.

Odd Numbers / Solo Patrol

If there is an odd number of units online, the highest ranking officer present may patrol independently. If you are the sole deputy on duty, solo patrol is authorized.
